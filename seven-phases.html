The 7 Phases of youth development

One national curriculum, delivered locally. Each phase builds on the one before it, and a Leaguer moves through all seven across his years in the program.

Phase IPhase IIPhase IIIPhase IVPhase VPhase VIPhase VII
I Phase I

Self-Identity & Purpose

Discipline, character, self-assurance and personal presentation. Every Leaguer begins by learning who he is and what he is accountable for.

  • Personal presentation and grooming standards
  • Character, discipline and accountability sessions
  • Goal setting and self-assessment
  • Chapter creed and program expectations
II Phase II

Training & Academic Preparation

Tutoring, study habits and college entrance preparation. Grades are tracked term over term, and mentors intervene early.

  • Structured tutoring and homework support
  • Study skills and time management
  • PSAT, SAT and ACT preparation
  • Term-over-term grade tracking with mentor follow-up
III Phase III

Competition & Preparedness

Public speaking, debate, academic bowls and sportsmanship. Leaguers compete at chapter, province and national levels.

  • Public speaking and oratorical competition
  • Debate and academic bowl teams
  • Province and national competition preparation
  • Sportsmanship and team conduct
IV Phase IV

Social & Cultural Awareness

Etiquette, fine arts and interpersonal communication. Young men learn to carry themselves in any room they enter.

  • Formal etiquette and dining instruction
  • Fine arts exposure and cultural outings
  • Interpersonal and written communication
  • Community service with cultural institutions
V Phase V

Health & Wellness Education

Physical fitness, mental health awareness and personal health literacy taught by qualified presenters.

  • Physical fitness programming
  • Mental health awareness with qualified presenters
  • Nutrition and personal health literacy
  • Age-appropriate decision-making instruction
VI Phase VI

Economic Empowerment

Financial literacy, budgeting, investing and entrepreneurship, taught with real accounts and real decisions.

  • Budgeting and banking fundamentals
  • Credit, saving and investing basics
  • Entrepreneurship projects and pitch practice
  • Understanding pay, taxes and first employment
VII Phase VII

College & Career Readiness

Campus tours, FAFSA assistance, resume workshops and sustained mentorship through the college application cycle.

  • College campus tours
  • FAFSA and scholarship application assistance
  • Resume, essay and interview workshops
  • One-to-one mentorship through application season
